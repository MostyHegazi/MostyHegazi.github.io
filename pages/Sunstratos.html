The Main Concept

Making a foldable glider which can do remote and dangerous missions at a low operation cost compared to any other functional alternative. 

My contribution

I conducted research into the properties and functionality of proposed communication systems and antennas. Additionally, I helped with designing the PCB which was used as an onboard avionics PCB. This circuit was launched in collaboration with Sunride Karman Alpha Rocket for testing purposes.
